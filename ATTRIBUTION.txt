The SEED project was funded by multiple grants from the US National Science Foundation.

The Flask applications under:
  provisioning/roles/iot-server/files/user_vm/        (IoT thermostat server)
  provisioning/roles/attacker-server/files/attacker_vm/ (attacker web server)
are the original SEED lab files (user_vm.zip / attacker_vm.zip), unchanged except
for one addition to iot.py: a /flag endpoint used only to let CyberRangeCZ verify,
per sandbox, that the DNS rebinding succeeded.
